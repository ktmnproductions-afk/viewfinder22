Three ways to frame it

Canon R50 · 18–150 mm
Wide landscape
18–24 mm

Use a foreground edge to lead into the town, coast or hills; keep the horizon deliberate.

City with context
35–70 mm

Balance the main subject with a band of surrounding landscape; avoid a postcard framing.

Compressed layers
100–150 mm

Use telephoto compression to stack rooftops, towers or ridgelines where visible.
