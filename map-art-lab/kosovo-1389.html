Date & place14TH CENTURY
15 June 1389 · Julian
The battle date is commonly preserved as 15 June in the Julian calendar. A strict proleptic Gregorian conversion is 23 June 1389. The modern Vidovdan anniversary falls on 28 June; many summaries retrospectively label that anniversary “28 June 1389,” which is not a strict calendar conversion.
