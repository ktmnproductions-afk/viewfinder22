Method & documentation

How to read this map.

This is a modern geographic base with interpretive historical overlays. It is not an authentic 1389 map, and no exact medieval frontline, troop count, manoeuvre path or death site is presented as established fact.

HISTORIOGRAPHY / BATTLE CONTEXT

Stephen W. Reinert, “Kosovo Polje, first battle of” — overview of the battle, its context and date traditions.

RETROSPECTIVE BATTLE MAP

“Skica bojnoga polja na Kosovu 1389” (1895), GeoPortOst / Leibniz Institute — a later reconstruction, useful as a historical cartographic artefact, not a contemporary source or proof of exact troop placement.

MODERN LANDSCAPE

Elevation is rendered from the public AWS Terrarium elevation-tile service. River lines are simplified modern geographic references and are not claims about medieval river courses. OpenStreetMap and its contributors provide geographic context for the named waterways and settlements.

CALENDAR NOTE

15 June 1389 is the commonly preserved Julian date; 23 June is its strict proleptic Gregorian equivalent. 28 June is the date of the modern Vidovdan anniversary and is often retroactively used in summaries.
