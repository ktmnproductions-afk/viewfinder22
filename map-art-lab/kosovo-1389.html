Art direction / documentation

An illustration, not a recovered battle map.

This view deliberately uses the visual language of a cinematic historical strategy-map illustration: a warm open field, wooded edges, small formations, banners, village clusters and bold movement arrows. It is not an authentic 1389 map.

The early evidence does not support confidently plotting exact troop numbers, formation boundaries, movement routes or death sites. Army placement, soldier density and arrows here are art-directed and schematic. Geographic labels are modern orientation points; the scene is not a scale-accurate terrain survey.

DATE

15 June 1389 is commonly preserved as the Julian date; its strict proleptic Gregorian equivalent is 23 June. The modern Vidovdan anniversary is 28 June.
