Geography / evidence / art direction

Grounded terrain. Qualified tactics.

The terrain mesh samples the public AWS Terrarium elevation tiles in the vicinity of Mazgit, Gazimestan, Obilić and Priština. Relief is mildly exaggerated for legibility. This is modern topography, not a reconstruction of medieval field boundaries or vegetation.

The Kosovo basin has a broad central plain and gentler surrounding relief. Published geographic descriptions note the Sitnica crossing the plateau, tributaries including the Lab in the north, and marshy ground in parts of the lower Lab area. The scene therefore avoids a uniformly steep or mountainous battlefield.

Army locations, formation widths, banners and arrows are interpretive scene design. Early accounts are fragmentary; surviving evidence does not support exact battle lines, unit counts, turn-by-turn routes or precise death locations. Phase three explicitly shows later tactical traditions as conjectural.
